FAQ

How is pricing determined?

Mostly volume, access, labor, and disposal fees. Heavy items or special handling can change the price.

Do you offer same-day service?

Often yes. We work daily 9-5 and fit urgent jobs in same-day or ASAP when the schedule allows.

Do you do light demolition?

Yes. Sheds, decks, hot tubs, playsets, carpet, and tile removal. We haul the debris and sweep the area.

What about hazardous materials?

We do not handle regulated hazardous waste, including paint, solvents, oils, pesticides, propane tanks, asbestos-containing materials, medical sharps, or appliances containing Freon. Call or text if you are unsure.
