Electronics

Outdoor

Renovation Debris

Misc.

Boxes, totes, bikes, exercise equipment, mixed junk.

Hazardous Materials

We do not handle regulated hazardous waste. Items that require licensed disposal must be arranged through the appropriate channel before scheduling. Call us if you are not sure whether something qualifies.

This includes paint, solvents, oils and antifreeze, batteries, fluorescent bulbs, pesticides, propane tanks, asbestos-containing materials, medical sharps, and appliances containing Freon. These items require separate disposal and are not included in a standard haul-away job.
