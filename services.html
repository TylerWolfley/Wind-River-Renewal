Hazardous Materials

We do not handle regulated hazardous waste. Items that require licensed disposal must be arranged through the appropriate channel before scheduling. Call us if you are not sure whether something qualifies.

This includes paint, solvents, oils and antifreeze, batteries, fluorescent bulbs, pesticides, propane tanks, asbestos-containing materials, medical sharps, and appliances containing Freon. These items require separate disposal and are not included in a standard haul-away job.

FAQ

How is pricing determined?

Mostly volume, access, and labor. Send photos for a fast estimate. Heavy items or special handling can change the price.

Do you offer same-day service?

Often yes. We work daily 9–5 and fit urgent jobs in same-day or ASAP when we can.

Do you take appliances and furniture?

Yes. Appliances, mattresses, couches, office furniture, and more. We donate when it makes sense.

Do you do light demolition?

Yes. Sheds, decks, hot tubs, playsets, plus carpet and tile removal. We haul the debris and leave it sweep-clean.

What payment methods do you take?
