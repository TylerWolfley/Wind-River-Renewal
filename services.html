Cleanouts & hauling

Room for a fresh start.

Whole-property cleanouts, estate cleanouts, and bundled hauling for homes, rentals, and properties changing hands.

From a full property cleanout to a single piece of furniture, we load what needs to go and protect what stays. Common loads include appliances, mattresses, couches, boxes, garage junk, storage unit contents, drywall, lumber, and renovation debris.

  • Furniture, appliances (including refrigerators and Freon-containing appliances), and mixed household junk
  • Garage, basement, storage, and estate cleanouts
  • Construction and renovation debris

Rental Turnovers

We clear what tenants leave behind so the unit can be cleaned, repaired, photographed, or rented again. This includes furniture, appliances, trash, dumped items, garage contents, and exterior debris.

  • Move-out and eviction cleanups
  • Apartment, house, and rental property cleanouts
  • Before and after photos when needed

Estate & Real Estate Cleanouts

Estate cleanouts, foreclosure cleanups, and listing-prep jobs need to be cleared without turning into another project. We haul the contents, sweep the area, and document the result when photos are needed.

  • Estate and foreclosure cleanouts
  • Listing prep for agents and asset managers
  • Combine rooms, garages, and outbuildings in one request

Exterior Cleanup

We clear brush, branches, old fencing, shed contents, dumped junk, and outdoor debris piles. This is useful before listing, after tenant move-outs, or when a yard has become too much to clean up by hand.

  • Brush, branches, yard waste, and debris
  • Fence panels and outdoor junk
  • Cleanup for rentals, resale, and curb appeal

Light Demolition

FAQ

How is pricing determined?

The total depends on volume, labor, access, travel, and disposal fees. Photos help us estimate; we may need more details or a site visit before confirming a firm price. Our service minimum is $150.

Do you offer same-day service?

Call or text with your location and timing needs. We will confirm availability after reviewing the job.

Do you do light demolition?

Yes. Sheds, decks, hot tubs, playsets, carpet, and tile removal. We haul the debris and sweep the area.

What about hazardous materials?

We do not accept paint, solvents, oils and antifreeze, batteries, fluorescent bulbs, pesticides, propane tanks, asbestos-containing materials, medical sharps, or other regulated hazardous waste. Refrigerators and other Freon-containing appliances are accepted. Tell us what you have so we can confirm the scope.
