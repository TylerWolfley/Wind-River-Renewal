About Wind River Renewal

Local, hands-on, and reliable property cleanup—junk removal, cleanouts, and light demolition done right.

Tyler Wolfley, Owner

My name is Tyler Wolfley, and I started Wind River Renewal because I kept seeing the same thing around Fremont County—good properties getting held back by mess, neglect, or leftover debris that nobody wanted to deal with.

This is an owner-operated company. When you call, you’re working directly with me.
