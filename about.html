About Wind River Renewal

Local, hands-on, and reliable property cleanup—junk removal, cleanouts, and light demolition done right.

Tyler Wolfley, Owner

My name is Tyler Wolfley, and I started Wind River Renewal because I kept seeing the same thing around Fremont County—good properties getting held back by mess, neglect, or leftover debris that nobody wanted to deal with. The work isn’t complicated, but it takes effort, care, and follow-through.

This is an owner-operated company. When you call, you’re working directly with me. I handle the scope, the schedule, and the finish—so you’re not chasing down a rotating crew or a middleman who never saw the property.

What we do

We focus on practical, real-world property work that makes a place usable again—whether it’s getting ready to sell, turning over a rental, cleaning up an estate, or clearing out a neglected lot.

  • Junk removal and full property cleanouts
  • Estate, foreclosure, and rental cleanups
  • Fire-damaged debris removal (non-structural)
  • Light demolition and tear-outs
  • Exterior debris cleanup and site clearing
  • Sale-ready property prep and maintenance

Licensed & insured — and we leave a clean, professional finish.

Trusted by Banks, Asset Managers & Real Estate Pros

Wind River Renewal works with homeowners, landlords, real estate agents, and property managers to get properties cleaned up and ready for whatever comes next—sale, repair, or turnover.

We understand the expectations that come with professional property work: clear communication, accurate scope, reliable scheduling, and documented results. If a listing needs a reset, an estate needs cleared, or a foreclosure needs cleanup, we handle it efficiently and leave it sale-ready.

  • Experienced with foreclosure & estate cleanouts
  • Responsive communication and clear timelines
  • Photo documentation available when needed
  • Licensed & insured
  • Local Fremont County operator
