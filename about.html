About Wind River Renewal

Local, hands-on, reliable property cleanup. Junk removal, cleanouts, and light demolition done right.

Tyler Wolfley, Owner

My name is Tyler Wolfley. I started Wind River Renewal because I kept seeing good properties held back by mess, neglect, or leftover debris that nobody wanted to deal with. That started in Fremont County, and we now serve Natrona County too.

This is an owner-operated company. When you call, you work directly with me.

What we do

  • Junk removal and full property cleanouts
  • Estate, foreclosure, and rental cleanups
  • Fire-damaged debris removal (non-structural)
  • Light demolition and tear-outs
  • Exterior debris cleanup and site clearing
  • Sale-ready property prep and maintenance

Licensed & insured and we leave a clean, professional finish.
