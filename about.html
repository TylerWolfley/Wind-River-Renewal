About Wind River Renewal

Owner-operated junk removal, property cleanouts, and light demolition serving Fremont County and Natrona County, Wyoming.

Owner-operated Licensed & insured Photo documentation Sweep-clean finish

Tyler Wolfley, Owner

My name is Tyler Wolfley. I started Wind River Renewal because I kept seeing good properties held back by mess, neglect, or leftover debris that nobody wanted to deal with. Properties that could sell or rent, but couldn't get there because of junk, old furniture, years of buildup, or demolition debris that just sat.

That problem started in Fremont County — Riverton and Lander are home base — and we now run jobs regularly in Natrona County and Casper too.

This is an owner-operated company. When you call or text, you work directly with me. I handle the quote, the schedule, and the job. There is no dispatcher between us.

What we do

  • Junk removal and full property cleanouts
  • Estate, foreclosure, and rental cleanups
  • Fire-damaged debris removal (non-structural)
  • Light demolition: sheds, decks, hot tubs, playsets
  • Carpet and tile removal and haul-away
  • Exterior debris cleanup and site clearing
  • Sale-ready property prep for agents and managers

Licensed & insured. We carry proper coverage. Banks, agents, and property managers can work with us without the liability question.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Wind River Renewal owner-operated?

Yes. Tyler Wolfley owns and runs the company. He handles every quote, every schedule, and every job. You deal with one person start to finish.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry proper coverage. This matters when you are putting a hauler on a property you own, manage, or are trying to sell. Banks and asset managers often require it.

What areas do you serve?

Fremont County and Natrona County are the core service area. That includes Riverton, Lander, Casper, Mills, Evansville, Shoshoni, Hudson, Pavillion, and surrounding communities. Not sure if you're covered? Call or text and we will let you know.

How do I get a fast quote?

Text photos, your address, and what you want removed to 307-349-4694. This gives us everything we need to price the job accurately. We reply fast.

Do you do foreclosure and estate cleanouts?

Yes. This is a large part of what we do. We work with real estate agents, property managers, banks, and estate executors. We provide before and after photos and leave a sweep-clean finish.

What do you not take?

We do not handle regulated hazardous waste: paint, solvents, oils, batteries, pesticides, propane tanks, asbestos, medical sharps, and Freon appliances. Call us if you are not sure and we will help you figure out the right path.
