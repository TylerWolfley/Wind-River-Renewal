Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a fast quote?

Text 1–3 photos, your address, and what needs gone to 307-349-4694. We’ll reply with price and availability.

Do you do foreclosure and estate cleanouts?

Yes. This is a large part of what we do. We work with real estate agents, property managers, banks, and estate executors. We provide before and after photos and leave a cleared work area.

What do you not take?

We do not accept paint, solvents, oils and antifreeze, batteries, fluorescent bulbs, pesticides, propane tanks, asbestos-containing materials, medical sharps, or other regulated hazardous waste. Refrigerators and other Freon-containing appliances are accepted. Tell us what you have so we can confirm the scope.
